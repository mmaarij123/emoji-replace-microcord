## Emoji artwork in the APKs

The artwork builder takes PNGs from DevilBro’s EmojiReplace project at the revision linked below, resizes and converts the images to WebP, and packages the results into an indexed image bank. Both APK variants contain the same 13 packs at different image sizes and compression levels. [Pinned EmojiReplace artwork source](https://github.com/mwittrien/BetterDiscordAddons/tree/9ea61dfba5599b1c8205d261fbbe35bf683120ab/Themes/EmojiReplace/emojis)

OpenMoji artwork is designed by the OpenMoji project and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The WebP versions have been resized and re-encoded. Preserve attribution, identify those changes, and follow the ShareAlike terms when sharing adapted OpenMoji artwork. [OpenMoji artwork license](https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji#license) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

The other packs are Apple, BlobMoji, Facebook, Google, Huawei, JoyPixels, Microsoft, Microsoft-3D, Samsung, Samsung-Old, Toss, and WhatsApp. Their artwork retains the terms of its respective rights holders. Check the terms for the artwork you intend to redistribute. The upstream source license is available at the revision used by the builder. [BetterDiscordAddons license](https://github.com/mwittrien/BetterDiscordAddons/blob/9ea61dfba5599b1c8205d261fbbe35bf683120ab/LICENSE)
